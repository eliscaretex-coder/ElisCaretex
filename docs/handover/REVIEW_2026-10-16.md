# Platform Review — 2026-10-16

**Last continuation update:** 2026-10-17

## Scope

This review covered the current frontend, migration history, live Supabase
configuration, production-flow screens and role-aware browser behaviour. No
passwords or other credentials are recorded here.

## Delivered

- MOP trolley size/count validation aligned with Finish behaviour.
- Terminal MOP ABS posting uses the signed-in workstation context correctly.
- Sorting links to the shared Production Tracker Operational List instead of a
  separately maintained embedded tracker.
- Not-started Production Tracker rows are visually subdued while active and
  completed work retains emphasis and route colour.
- Distribution Daily Route Board shows current and next delivery dates, official
  Finish/MOP production, physical trolley count and known load.
- Incomplete Staff, Driver and Fleet master data is visible as a warning without
  becoming a blanket save requirement.
- Mobile Driver cards retain their incomplete-data warning.
- Terminal home pages skip the notification-summary RPC when the account does
  not have Notifications permission, avoiding a harmless but noisy console error.
- Distribution Route Master now saves new routes reliably from its explicit Save
  button.
- Distribution workspace tabs are visible again on desktop. Drivers, Fleet,
  Routes and the other Distribution views are no longer reachable only through
  the mobile layout.
- Staff `Accounts & access` and the Customer Master/Operational Reports tabs are
  visible again on desktop. They had been hidden by the compact app-shell rule.
- The Customer Weekly Planner now calls the schedule value `Expected KG`, gives
  every missing row a direct `Set KG` action, and explains the exact Edit
  Schedule path to the per-day Expected KG field.
- The editable customer schedule shows all six operating days in one desktop
  row, three on medium screens and one on mobile. Order and delivery are compact
  indicators, while Expected KG remains visually separated
  from Production Order, instructions and trolley requirements.
